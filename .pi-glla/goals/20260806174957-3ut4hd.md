## Objective

> Add fail-fast quota: heartbeat-without-progress detection. Add a watchdog in the auditor process that detects heartbeat-without-progress: when `lastActivityAt` is fresh but no new `toolCall`/`recentOutput` arrives within a bounded window, demote the auditor to `quiet` and auto-cancel the detached job (steal-list #7).

## Verification contract

- (a) the watchdog in `extensions/goal-loop-auditor-process.ts` emits `auditor_stalled` and cancels the job
- (b) a test in `tests/auditor-process.test.ts` pins the cancel
- (c) full suite green and `npx tsc --noEmit` clean
